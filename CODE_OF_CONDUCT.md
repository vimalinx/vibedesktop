# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate,
disagree with ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and make
space for participants with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to chesten@163.com. Reports will be reviewed
in confidence. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful to the
community. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
